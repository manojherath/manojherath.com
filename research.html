Research

My research focuses on Digital Twin systems, Cloud-Edge-IoT architectures, software architecture, model-driven engineering, and intelligent distributed systems.

I am particularly interested in the design, modeling, deployment, and runtime management of distributed Digital Twin systems across heterogeneous computing environments.
